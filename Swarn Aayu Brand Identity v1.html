01 — The Mark

Three directions.
One question.

The logomark should feel like a question being asked — never an answer delivered. Three explorations, each drawing from a different Indian and intellectual metaphor. All rooted in the same core: inquiry, agency, illumination.

Direction A · Preferred
The Inquiry Arc
An open circle — the question mid-flight. Two concentric arcs suggest depth; the terminal dot is potential, not punctuation.
Direction B
The Diya
The oil lamp as mark of care. Rooted in Indian visual tradition, distilled to pure geometry. Illumination without excess.
Direction C
The Triad
Three nodes, one network. Seniors, children, adults — bound by a shared conviction. The centre point is the foundation itself.

The texture of inquiry.

08 — Brand Voice

Write like you think.

The brand voice mirrors the philosophy — it asks more than it tells. It uses short sentences. It does not over-explain. It never uses the passive voice when the active will do. It trusts the reader's intelligence. It knows that the question is often more powerful than the answer.

Principle Not this This
Questions over answers Aayu provides seniors with answers to their daily questions. What if every senior had someone who truly listened?
Earned brevity We leverage cutting-edge artificial intelligence to enable senior citizens to achieve digital independence. Built so seniors can go at their own pace. On their own terms.
Honest restraint Our revolutionary AI transforms lives and disrupts the status quo. This is not intelligence. It's a mirror. We build something different.
