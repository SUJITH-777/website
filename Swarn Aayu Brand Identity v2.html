The mark & its meaning.

The logomark is a hybrid: the outer arc draws from the AI tradition of open inquiry — a question in flight, never closed. The inner triad draws from the yantra tradition — three spokes, three initiatives, three audiences, radiating from a single bindu. The terminal dot is potential, not punctuation.

The Arc
An open circle — a question in motion. The AI tradition of inquiry: never closed, always pending resolution.
The Triad
Three spokes from the bindu — the yantra's three forces. Here: Aayu, Curiosity Coach, Thinking Matters.
The Bindu
The origin point — the foundation itself. In yantra philosophy, the bindu is where all creation begins.
